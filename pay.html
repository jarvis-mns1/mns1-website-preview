The Tax Angle Most Carriers Skip

Per Diem Saves You $2,860/Year

Most carriers pay a flat rate — and every penny gets taxed. At MNS1, $0.10 of your $0.60/mile is classified as per diem — non-taxable under IRS rules. That's real money the government doesn't touch.

The Math

$0.10/mile × 2,500 miles/week = $250/week untaxed
$250/week × 52 weeks = $13,000/year in per diem
At 22% marginal tax rate: $2,860/year in tax savings

A carrier paying $0.60/mile flat rate? Every dollar gets taxed. MNS1's $0.50 base + $0.10 per diem structure means you keep more of what you earn. Same highway, different tax bill.
